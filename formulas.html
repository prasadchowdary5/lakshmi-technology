Introduction to Excel

Understand the fundamentals of Excel before diving into formulas and functions

What is Excel?

Microsoft Excel is a powerful spreadsheet application used for data organization, analysis, and visualization. It's part of the Microsoft Office suite and is essential for business, finance, and data analysis.

Cells, Rows & Columns

Cell: The basic unit of a worksheet where data is entered (e.g., A1, B5).
Row: Horizontal group of cells (numbered 1, 2, 3...).
Column: Vertical group of cells (lettered A, B, C...).

Selection & Criteria

Selection: Highlighting cells to perform operations.
Criteria: Conditions used in functions like COUNTIF, SUMIF.
K: Position parameter in functions like LARGE, SMALL.
Array: Collection of data values arranged in rows and columns.
